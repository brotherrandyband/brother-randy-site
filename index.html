Meet Brother Randy

BROTHER RANDY is from Douglasville, GA. The members grew up down the street from each other and are now homeowners. They started this band to have an excuse to hang out together.

They make loud music about bad decisions, complicated relationships, and other problems you'd think they'd be old enough to avoid by now.

BROTHER RANDY sounds like growing up in the South, discovering emo, and never fully recovering from either.
